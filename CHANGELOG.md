# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First release. Built and tested against 7 Days to Die V 3.2.0 (b9).

- Quality tier 7 for 43 weapons, 16 tools, 66 armour pieces, 12 clothing pieces and 35 item mods.
- Every tier-scaled number continues at the step vanilla was already using - damage, durability,
  resists, ranges, harvest counts.
- One extra mod slot over tier 6.
- Armour set bonuses and item mod effects continue at tier 7.
- Crafting a tier 7 costs six Legendary Parts where tier 6 costs one.
- Tier 7 drops five times less often than tier 6, and traders never stock it.
- Crafting skill caps raised to 60, 90 and 120 across all sixteen crafting skills, with tier 7
  sitting on the new cap.
- Twenty junk items - pipe guns, primitive weapons, stone tools, primitive armour - get tier 7 free
  and gain no extra mod slot.
- Gold quality band for tier 7, and `#7` in the creative menu search box.
